## Retrieval audit debt

The v0.3 shortlist is deliberately bounded and normally avoids exhaustive scoring. A focused diagnostic compared its top 25 with exhaustive exact reranking for a difficult `orange` Pivot query. Exact identity recovery was **8/25**, while mean score regret was approximately **0.0031**. The low regret suggests the shortlist found similarly scored alternatives; the identity recovery still fails the roadmap's membership-recall ambition and can hide a specific useful option. Treat indexed retrieval as a v0.3 prototype until recall is audited across a frozen representative query set and final latency is measured.

## Validation boundary

No rapper, lyricist, dialect reviewer, or other human judge has yet reviewed the current recommendation set. Nothing in the automated suite establishes usefulness, dialect authenticity, similarity to a named artist, or improvement over a conventional rhyme dictionary. RhymeGraph uses no scraped lyrics and is not designed to imitate an artist; artist/song references used during product critique motivated vocabulary and failure-case audits only.
